# Project AI Context: Canonical Identity Service

> **Overview:** CIS is a unified identity and authorization service that acts as the single source of truth for user/member records across the Harvesters digital estate (Faith Hub, Reporting System, Church CRM, DMHicc, MyHarvestHub). Built with Prisma + Supabase PostgreSQL, it features config-driven roles, async event broadcasting, and complete audit trails. Execution: 4 phases in 10 days (May 7–16).
